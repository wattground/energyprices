Calculation conventions & limitations

One daily accrual timeline. Source dates identify period starts. Annualized rates use actual calendar days / 365 or 366; period amounts are spread uniformly over covered days. Weekly index values therefore imply constant daily revenue within each week. The final observation's inclusive end date is editable. Gaps are excluded and reported; they are never filled with zero.

Contract settlement. Annual and monthly prices accrue using actual days in their calendar year or month. Floors settle on calendar months or years: F + α × max(M − F, 0). F is the prorated guarantee for observed days; M is merchant revenue on that tranche. The signed floor adjustment is allocated uniformly over those observed days. Monthly floor charts with annual settlement therefore reflect the entire selected annual bucket. Swap accruals sum to the selected daily, monthly or annual settlement; payment delays are not modeled.

Incomplete history. Partial settlement buckets use prorated fixed payments and floors, explicitly assuming settlement on observed days only. Changing the date window recalculates floors. Annual risk indicators use only fully covered calendar years; variability requires at least two. Missing TB4 blocks active variable swaps. Negative swap settlements and negative merchant components are retained. Percentage changes use Δ / |merchant|, and are undefined when merchant is zero.

Historical revenue comparison. Proportional allocation of historical merchant revenue is an analytical assumption. Actual dispatch under multiple contracts, FCA restrictions, cycling limits and shared SoC could produce different outcomes. An index is not evidence of realized owner cash receipts. No forecast, bankability assessment, DSCR, debt capacity or equity IRR is calculated. Fees, taxes, degradation and counterparty default are not added unless already reflected in the imported series.
